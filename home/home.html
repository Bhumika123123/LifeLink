ABOUT LIFELINK

Connecting People. Saving Lives.

What is LifeLink?

LifeLink is a platform designed to make the donation process easier, more organized, and more accessible.

Our platform brings together three important participants: Donors, Patients, and Hospitals.

Donors can register their information and indicate what they are willing to donate. Hospitals and patients can submit requests for the donations they need.

LifeLink helps create a connection between people who are willing to help and those who need support.
